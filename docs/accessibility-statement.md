# Accessibility Statement

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Welicare is built for people living in care homes, many of them with dementia, and for the family, staff and volunteers who support them. We want everyone to be able to use the app comfortably, whatever their sight, hearing, movement or memory.

## Our commitment

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** in the Welicare app and on welicare.ca. Accessibility is part of how we design and test new features, not something added at the end. We have reviewed the app against these guidelines ourselves, and we are still working towards full conformance (see the known limitations below).

## Features that support accessibility

- **VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android):** buttons, photos and game pieces have spoken labels. Menus and pop-ups keep the screen reader's focus inside them until they close. PIN entry tells you how many digits you've entered.
- **Larger text (Dynamic Type):** text follows the size you choose in your device's settings.
- **Reduce Motion:** when Reduce Motion is on in your device settings, the games skip bouncing and pulsing animations and show changes straight away.
- **Colour contrast:** text and controls use colours chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast (at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and controls). Information is never shown by colour alone.
- **Large touch targets:** buttons are at least 44 × 44 points. Resident Mode's Back and Home buttons and the music controls are larger again.
- **Readable fonts:** we use Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low-vision readers.
- **Games with no pressure:** resident games have no timers counting down, no scores that go down, no "game over" and no "wrong" messages. Moving pieces never count against the player.
- **Your own Play/Pause button:** the music player has a large Play/Pause button of its own, so you don't have to rely on YouTube's small controls.

## Known limitations

- **YouTube player controls:** the video player itself comes from YouTube. Its built-in controls are small and are labelled by YouTube, not by us.
- **Very large text sizes:** at the largest text settings, some text inside fixed-size areas (such as the number keypad and game cards) may be cut off. We are still testing this.
- **Some text in Resident Mode is smaller than we'd like:** song and movie titles and a few labels are below our 24-point goal for resident screens.
- **Screen-reader testing:** we have not yet finished testing every screen with VoiceOver on iPhone and iPad.

We are working on these and will update this statement as they are fixed.
